# 🦓 Zoo Animal Management System

A centralized digital platform for managing animal records, medical history, feeding, habitat, and institutional transfers in zoological facilities.

## 👥 Stakeholders
- **Veterinarians** – Manage medical documentation and treatments.  
- **Animal Keepers** – Record daily behavior and health observations.  
- **Registrars** – Handle administrative data and animal transfers.

## 🧩 Core Features
- **Animal Records** – Identification, lineage, weight, and behavioral data.  
- **Medical History** – Vaccinations, surgeries, medications, and preventive care.  
- **Feeding & Nutrition** – Species-specific diet plans and feeding schedules.  
- **Observations** – Daily keeper logs and health alerts.  
- **Habitat Management** – Environmental tracking and seasonal adjustments.  
- **Transfers** – Outgoing/incoming records with permits and shipment tracking.

## 🎯 Goal
Ensure accurate, exportable, and compliant animal data to support welfare, research, and inter-zoo coordination.
